# 05_quality_assessment

Quality assessment of the 182 synthesised studies on the five items of Dybå and Dingsøyr: QA1 clear objective, QA2 experimental setup described, QA3 comparison against a baseline, QA4 limitations discussed, QA5 conclusions supported by the evidence. Each item was scored 0, 0.5 or 1.0 from the full text of the paper. The procedure is described in Section 4.6 of the paper.

`pdf_qa_scores.csv` holds the per-paper scores. Mean total 4.14, minimum 2.5, maximum 5.0. Distribution: 31 x 5.0, 62 x 4.5, 40 x 4.0, 27 x 3.5, 19 x 3.0, 3 x 2.5. No paper fell below the 2.0 sensitivity threshold.
