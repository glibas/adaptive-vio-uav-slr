# 03_retrieval

Full-text retrieval of the 309 sought reports. `retrieval_status.csv` lists each report with `pdf_retrieved` (Y, or `N (EXC-8)` when no channel yielded the full text) and `in_final_corpus`.

283 reports were retrieved.

26 could not be obtained through any channel and were excluded under EXC-8 (13 of them Springer DOIs, 4 IEEE DOIs; 13 published 2025–2026). The PDFs themselves are not redistributed in this repository.
