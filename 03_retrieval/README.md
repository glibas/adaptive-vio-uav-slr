# 03_retrieval

Full-text retrieval of the 230 sought reports (May 2026). `retrieval_status.csv` lists each report with `pdf_retrieved` (Y, or `N (EXC-8)` when no channel yielded the full text) and `in_final_corpus`.

206 reports were retrieved.

24 could not be obtained through any channel and were excluded under EXC-8 (5 of them IEEE DOIs, 20 published 2023–2026). The PDFs themselves are not redistributed in this repository.
