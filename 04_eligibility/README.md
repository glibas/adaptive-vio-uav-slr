# 04_eligibility

Full-text triage of the 283 retrieved reports. `triage_worksheet.csv` records, per report: the thematic class (C1–C7), the three hard filters (`t1` algorithmic contribution, `t2` aerial validation, `t3` quantitative evaluation), the six relevance dimensions (`r1`–`r6`), the decision, the exclusion code and a note with the supporting quotation. Studies retained under the second clause of T-1 are identified in the notes ("second clause" or "T-1 borderline").

101 reports were excluded at this stage: 76 failed T-1 (25 of these also failed T-3), 13 failed T-2, 7 failed T-3 alone, 2 under the language criterion (INC-4), and 3 under EXC-9 (institutional origin, a non-scientific criterion applied after the T-filters). All three EXC-9 reports pass T-1/T-2/T-3. Including them would raise the corpus to 185 and change seven counts by one to three studies each, without changing which cells of the taxonomy are populated (Section 4.5 of the paper). The remaining 182 reports form the final synthesis corpus. Rows with `pdf_present = False` carry the abstract-level filter assessments of the unretrieved reports, where one was made, and are not part of the triage denominator.

`r1` (GNSS-denied operation) and `r2` (adaptive or online component) are the two synthesis dimensions reported in Table 6 of the paper: 125 of the 182 studies have `r1 = 1`, 140 have `r2 = 1`, and 96 have both. `r2` is determined at extraction (protocol, Section 6).
