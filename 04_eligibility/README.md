# 04_eligibility

Full-text triage of the 206 retrieved reports. `triage_worksheet.csv` records, per report: the thematic class (C1–C7), the three hard filters (`t1` algorithmic contribution, `t2` aerial validation, `t3` quantitative evaluation), the six relevance dimensions (`r1`–`r6`), the decision, the exclusion code and a note with the supporting quotation. Studies retained under the second clause of T-1 are marked `T-1 borderline` in the notes.

83 reports were excluded at this stage: 66 failed T-1 (23 of these also failed T-3), 7 failed T-2, 5 failed T-3 alone, 2 under the language criterion (INC-4), and 3 under EXC-9 (institutional origin, applied after the T-filters). All three EXC-9 reports pass T-1/T-2/T-3, and including them would raise the corpus to 126 without changing any conclusion of the review. The remaining 123 reports form the final synthesis corpus. Rows with `pdf_present = False` carry the abstract-level filter assessments of the unretrieved reports and are not part of the triage denominator.
